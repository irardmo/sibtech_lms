Employee Evaluation Form

Mastery of the subject matter:

Taught without reading notes.
Provide examples to illustrate difficult terms or concept.
Gave accurate answers to student's questions.
Related the topic to real-life situations.
Related the subject matter to other fields.
The day's lesson was drawn from the curriculum guide/syllabus.

Communication Skills:

Used correct grammar in speaking (English or Tagalog).
Maintained eye contact with the students.
Considered and used students' ideas and suggestions.
Asking probing questions.
Spoke in a voice that is clear and loud enough to be heard by everyone.

Classroom Management:

Seating arrangement was in accordance with the seat plan.
Orderliness and cleanliness of the classroom was maintained.
Discipline was observed among the students.
Instructional materials were in placed.
The over-all atmosphere of the classroom is conducive to learning.

Reminder:

Provide an evaluation for all of your teachers from 1st Semester A.Y. 2025-2026. If you have already completed the evaluation, please skip or do not answer the next evaluation forms.
